How to play

One round takes one to two minutes.

  1. Pick a mapChoose Arena, School or City under Practice map. In a room, everyone votes instead.
  2. Find the red ballIt floats over whoever is IT. If it is above you, chase. If not, run.
  3. Pass the tagIT touches another player and they become IT. The new IT is frozen for 1.5 seconds.
  4. Stay safeYou earn a point for every second you are not IT. Most points when time runs out wins.

Use the freezeWhen you tag someone, run the other way straight away. They can't move for 1.5 seconds and can't tag you back for 3.5.
Corners beat straight linesOn School and City the tigers path around walls. Cut through doors and alleys to break their line of sight.
Sprint in burstsSprinting uses stamina and it comes back slowly. Save it for when IT gets close.
Stay away from the edgesOn the Arena a tiger can pin you against the fence. Circle around the middle instead.

Questions

How many people can play together?

Up to eight players share a room.

What is the red ball?

It floats over whoever is IT. If it is above you, chase someone. If it is above someone else, run.

Why does Multiplayer take so long to connect?

The game server sleeps when nobody is using it. The first player to connect wakes it up, which can take up to a minute.

Can I play on a phone or with a controller?

Yes. Phones get an on-screen joystick and sprint button, and a controller works with the left stick for moving and the right stick for the camera.

Does getting tagged end my round?

No. Every round runs the full time. Being IT just stops your safe seconds from counting.

How is the winner decided?

Everyone earns a point for each second they are not IT. When the clock runs out, the highest score wins and ties are shown as ties.
